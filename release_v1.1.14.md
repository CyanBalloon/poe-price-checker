## Search & Compare

Item Search keeps matches and selected prices side by side in PoE1 and PoE2. Select a match again to remove it, or keep adding items to the comparison as you search. Selections are kept separately for each game. Prices use the matching game’s currency artwork, including Chaos and Divine Orbs in PoE1 and Exalted and Divine Orbs in PoE2.

## Official Currency Exchange prices

Exchange items use completed hourly trades from Grinding Gear Games’ public feed for the selected league. This includes Originator’s Exalted Orb, divination cards, and PoE2 uncut gems. Items with no recent exchange quote show that directly.

## Modifier selection

Each modifier group has a checkbox that shows whether none, some, or all of its filters are selected. Use it to select or clear the whole group. Implicit modifiers start unselected in the default price checks.

Update from **Settings → Application → Check for updates**.
