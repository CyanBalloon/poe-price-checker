## Viscera price checks for PoE1 3.30

Use **Ctrl+D** or **Paste item** on a Viscera to see its rolled modifiers in the **Implicit** group. Trade searches use the base and tier that Grinding Gear Games recognizes and include the selected implicit. A Flaming Furnace with 16% Fire Exposure and a Greater Defaced Bust with a 98% Endurance Charge chance now both work.

**Item Search** includes all 164 Viscera bases and their 426 named forms. Selecting a Viscera shows its body slot and implicit range. When a form has multiple possible modifiers, choose the implicit to see the estimate for that variant in your league.

The catalogue uses [Grinding Gear Games’ trade items and stats](https://www.pathofexile.com/api/trade/data/items), [PoEDB’s Viscera data](https://poedb.tw/us/Viscera), [Awakened PoE Trade’s 3.30 stat translations](https://github.com/SnosMe/awakened-poe-trade/releases/tag/v3.30.101), and [poe.ninja’s Viscera market data](https://poe.ninja/docs/api).

Update the installed or portable Windows edition from **Settings → Application → Check for updates**, then choose **Restart to update**.
