## PoE1 map price checks

Price checking a map now matches its tier exactly and uses its copied drop bonuses as editable minimums. Atlas Drops, item rarity, monster pack size, item quantity, and bonuses to Maps, Scarabs, Currency, and Divination Cards each use their own trade filter.

Maps with Originator’s Memories, Shaper or Elder influence, an Elder Guardian, or a Conqueror citadel start with that influence selected. Ordinary prefixes and suffixes stay folded and optional by default, including T1 rolls. Monster level is available as a separate filter. Listing rows show the map tier and Atlas Drops for a quick comparison.

## Item previews and filters

PoE1 socketed previews load the official socket and link artwork directly from GGG’s CDN and have a built-in offline fallback. Base, Implicit, and Pseudo stats start folded, with their selected counts visible.

Update from **Settings → Application → Check for updates**.
