## Foulborn uniques in PoE1

Price Check recognizes Foulborn uniques, including Foulborn Hand of Wisdom and Action. Replacement rolls appear in their own collapsible **Foulborn modifiers** group. Searchable Foulborn rolls start selected, and **Open trade** keeps the search restricted to Foulborn listings.

## Compact toolbar

The Mageblood artwork scales with the app title, and the desktop toolbar is back to 56 pixels tall.

Update from **Settings → Application → Check for updates**.
