## Price checking unidentified uniques

Press **Ctrl+D** or paste an unidentified unique into Price Check. Choose the matching artwork to open that unique in **Item Search**. For an unidentified **Marble Amulet**, the choices are **Bloodgrip** and **Bonemeld**.

In PoE1, **Check listings** searches for the chosen unique with the unidentified filter applied. It keeps the item level, sockets, and visible implicits from the original clipboard. Item Search displays the league’s market estimate when one is available.

The picker uses the current game's catalogue in both PoE1 and PoE2. It includes alternate bases and marks legacy entries where Grinding Gear Games distinguishes them. The base mappings come from [PoE1 trade data](https://www.pathofexile.com/api/trade/data/items) and [PoE2 trade data](https://www.pathofexile.com/api/trade2/data/items). Additional item artwork comes from the trade listings, [PoEDB](https://poedb.tw/us/Unique_item), and [PoE2DB](https://poe2db.tw/us/Unique_item).

Update the Windows app from **Settings → Application → Check for updates**.
