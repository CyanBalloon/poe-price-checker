## PoE1 Timeless Jewels

- Price Check selects the jewel's exact seed and named figure by default. An Elegant Hubris with Caspiro and seed 134800 searches for that exact combination.
- The seed field can be edited or unchecked to broaden the search. The market estimate is labeled **All seeds**, while trade listings use the selected filters.
- Supports basic and advanced clipboard text for the six Timeless Jewels and the Abyssal Historic Jewels, including legacy named variants.

Update from **Settings → Application → Check for updates** in either Windows edition.
