## Price Check filter defaults

- **PoE1** leaves the white-socket filter unchecked. You can still turn it on for an individual search.
- **PoE1 and PoE2** automatically select recognised tier 1 prefixes and suffixes when the clipboard includes advanced modifier details. The individual modifier is selected even when it also contributes to a pseudo stat or equipment total.

Update from **Settings → Application → Check for updates**.
