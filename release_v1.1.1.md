## The Windows auto-clicker is now opt-in

The auto-clicker starts **off** on new installations and when upgrading from an earlier release. To enable it, open **Settings → Shortcuts → Auto-clicker** and confirm the warning that repeated clicks may violate the Path of Exile Terms of Use. Your choice is saved for both PoE1 and PoE2.

Turning it off restores normal Ctrl and Shift right-clicks immediately. You can continue using the price-check hotkey with the auto-clicker off.
