# How to price check items in Path of Exile 1

Open [PoE Price Checker for Windows](/#download), select **PoE 1** and choose the PC league where you trade. Hover over an item in Path of Exile and press **Ctrl+D**. If you have already copied the item, select **Paste item** on the welcome screen.

## Read the chaos and divine prices

The PoE1 market estimate uses **Chaos Orbs** for items worth less than one Divine Orb. At one divine or more, the app shows the value in **Divine Orbs** first and the chaos equivalent below. The exchange rate in the toolbar is for the selected league.

[![Polaric Devastation with an estimate of 2.06 Divine Orbs, approximately 373 Chaos Orbs, and a list of asking prices](/images/poe1-price.webp)](/images/poe1-price.webp)

In this capture, **Polaric Devastation** had an estimate of **2.06 divines**, or about **373 chaos**. With its critical strike roll selected, the search found listings at **2 divines**. The estimate comes from poe.ninja. The listings show what sellers are asking for rings that match the filter. Both values change as the league market moves.

## Choose which item modifiers to compare

For a rare pair of boots, start with the rolls a buyer would want, such as movement speed and life. Expand **Prefix** or **Suffix**, select those rolls, and choose **Search listings**. If the results are too narrow, loosen a minimum or clear a modifier and choose **Apply filters**.

Use **Base** when a defence or weapon property matters. Expand **Pseudo stats** when an overall value such as total elemental resistance is more useful than an individual resistance roll. **Crafted** and **Fractured** appear only on items with the corresponding modifiers. Each group shows its selected count, even when folded.

## Look up an item without a clipboard copy

Open **Item Search** and type an item or currency name. Add or remove matches on the left as you compare their prices on the right. Items on the Currency Exchange use completed hourly trades from the selected league.
