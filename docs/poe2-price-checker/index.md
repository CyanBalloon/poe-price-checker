# How to price check items in Path of Exile 2

Open [PoE Price Checker for Windows](/#download), choose **PoE 2** and select your league. Hover over an item in game and press **Ctrl+D**. The item determines which view opens. Rare equipment opens in **Price Check**. Currency and identified uniques open in **Item Search**.

[![Path of Exile 2 selected on the welcome screen with the league selector and exchange rate in the toolbar](/images/poe2-welcome.webp)](/images/poe2-welcome.webp)

## Compare a rare weapon with trade listings

For a bow, start in **Base** and select a weapon property that matters to your build, such as physical DPS. If a particular roll adds value, expand **Prefix** or **Suffix** to include it. A fractured roll has its own **Fractured** group. Selecting fewer requirements gives you more comparable bows.

Choose **Search listings** to see asking prices in your PoE2 league. After changing a minimum or adding a modifier, choose **Apply filters** to refresh the matches. **Trade** opens the search on the official trade site.

## Check uniques and currency in Item Search

An identified unique opens in **Item Search** with its league estimate when one is available. Choose **Check rolls** on a unique copied from the game to compare its actual modifiers. When you look up a unique by name, use **Check listings** to search for that item.

For currency, type the orb’s name and select the result. The toolbar shows the Divine Orb exchange rate for the selected league when market data is available. If you also play PoE1, switching games restores that game’s own league and currency data.
