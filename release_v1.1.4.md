## PoE1 3.30 Item Search

Item Search now includes 6,233 PoE1 catalogue entries. You can find Hunter’s Exalted Orb, Merrick’s Ducat, and Covens items such as Flaming Goat Hoof as soon as the app opens. Search accepts straight or curly apostrophes and works with the localized item names in the bundled game data.

Price checking an item from the in-game Currency Exchange opens **Item Search** with that item selected. This works with **Ctrl+D** and **Paste item** in both games. Estimates update when prices arrive for your league. Items without a market quote show **No estimate**.

To update the installed or portable Windows edition from v1.1.3, open **Settings → Application → Check for updates**, then choose **Restart to update** when the download finishes.

The PoE1 catalogue combines [Grinding Gear Games’ trade items](https://www.pathofexile.com/api/trade/data/items) with [Awakened PoE Trade’s Covens data](https://github.com/SnosMe/awakened-poe-trade/releases/tag/v3.30.101). Exchange membership also uses the game data published by [PoEDB](https://poedb.tw/us/Currency_Exchange) and [PoE2DB](https://poe2db.tw/us/Currency_Exchange). League estimates come from [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/docs/api).
