## Price Check

- PoE1 trade searches use the correct implicit modifier for corrupted rolls such as **+1 to Maximum Frenzy Charges** on Volkuur’s Guidance. Select the roll under **Implicit** and apply the filters.
- Map and waystone searches offer **Corrupted**, **Uncorrupted**, and **Any**. The filter starts with the copied item’s corruption state.
- Uninfluenced PoE1 map searches exclude Shaper, Elder, the four Conqueror citadels, and Originator’s Memories. Influenced maps search for their own influence, including Conqueror maps copied without advanced modifier text.

## Auto-clicker

Repeat clicks require both Ctrl and the right mouse button. Releasing either stops the repetition, and holding both starts it again.

Update from **Settings → Application → Check for updates**.
