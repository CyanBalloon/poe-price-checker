## PoE1 item animations

- Item previews use your local PoE1 installation to render effects for supported items, including influenced and fractured gear, Memory Strands, and synthesized uniques such as Circle of Fear.
- The first animated preview builds an index of your game files. Later launches reuse the encrypted index until your game files change.
- The game artwork comes from your installed client at runtime. The downloads include the effect renderer and shader tools.

## Settings and price checks

- Application and updates are at the top of Settings. Appearance, Game & trade, and Shortcuts each have their own section. Atlas Observatory is the Settings background in both games.
- PoE1 item animations are on by default. Use the switch under **Appearance** for static previews, or hover the information icon for details.
- Switching between price checks keeps the checker visible. The Windows price-check hotkey keeps keyboard focus on Path of Exile.

In either Windows edition, open **Settings → Application → Check for updates**. Download the update if prompted, then choose **Restart to update**.
