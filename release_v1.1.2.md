## Update directly from the app

Open **Settings → Application** to check for a new version, download it, and choose **Restart to update**. Both Windows editions keep your settings. The portable edition also keeps its filename and location. With automatic updates enabled, the download happens in the background.

Installed v1.1.1 users can get this release from Settings with automatic updates enabled. If you use portable v1.1.1 or earlier, replace the executable once with the [v1.1.2 portable edition](https://github.com/CyanBalloon/poe-price-checker/releases/download/v1.1.2/poe-price-checker-1.1.2.exe). Future updates happen inside the app.

## Price checking and client logs

Restored PoE2 pseudo filters for resistance, attributes, life, movement speed, and tablet uses. Socketed augments stay separate. Campaign log parsing now accepts Windows line endings. The release build runs the full renderer test suite before packaging.
