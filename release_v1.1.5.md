## A quieter update notification

When PoE Price Checker finds a new version, a small notice appears in the bottom-right corner. Click it to jump to the update controls under **Settings → Application**, or click **×** to dismiss it. It disappears after 15 seconds. Download progress for the same version won’t bring it back.

The installed and portable Windows editions can update from v1.1.4 in **Settings → Application → Check for updates**. Once the download finishes, choose **Restart to update**.
